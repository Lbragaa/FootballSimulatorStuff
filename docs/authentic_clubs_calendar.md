## Registry, eligibility and strengths

`multiseason/club_catalogue.py` proposes 276 real clubs across the ten associations, including 163 Brazilian clubs and all 27 state federations. It documents state, country and explicit aliases. The installer reuses matched historical keys, existing strength and roster. New keys derive from a versioned country/state/name identity. Alias ambiguity or a conflicting recorded nationality stops installation. Missing country is supplied for forward selection through the catalogue without replacing historical source rows. The club lists use actual identities. Slots, registration, squad ratings and participation are fictional forward proposals. ## Player names and history

`world/player_names.py` generates one or two given names and surnames, with local and shared language pools. About 88% of draws use the host nationality and the rest use another South American pool. This is a modeled diversity parameter. Identity and nationality are stored separately from display text. The deterministic generator prevents duplicate names within each generated squad or youth intake. New youth use the same policy. Transfers preserve player IDs and name provenance.

Existing modeled players with a generic label on a reused genuine club get a separate 2029+ display alias keyed by player ID. The alias follows the player through transfers and feeds future match inputs and offseason narration. Their historical snapshots, stored names, results and numeric identities stay intact. Existing recorded players receive no alias. Older roster views that read the original database name directly can still show it.

## Fixture sequencing

The old circle used parity of both round and pairing index. For rotating clubs those changes could cancel, causing 19 straight home or away designations. The new scheme alternates the pivot by round and reverses the non-pivot pairs by pairing parity. Across tested sizes 4, 8, 20 and 32 it retains every ordered pair and limits runs to three. The national scheduler applies it only when a future-season precommit exists, before the first national fixture. Rest, venue and international-window checks continue through the existing calendar. Continental group draws and historic schedules use their existing policies.
