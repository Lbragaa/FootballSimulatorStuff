The commissioner must explicitly acknowledge these uncertainties:

- **Second yellows:** the fixed 1,200-trial analysis produced 306 dismissals,
  including 225 second yellows; 272 matches had a dismissal. Repeat cautions drive
  the high rate. Historical coverage cannot establish a reliable replacement rate.
- **Recovery:** 1.5 fitness points/day and squad rotation can keep deep squads
  near the fitness ceiling. Fitness is an availability score, not medical evidence;
  training, travel and foreign domestic workloads are incomplete.
- **Foreign squads:** quality/depth and missing biographical/registration facts are
  explicit fictional modeling assumptions, not reconstructed real-world history.
- **Injuries:** the current rare, fixed five-day absence model simplifies severity
  and recovery. It is not a comprehensive medical simulation.
- **Strength and tactics:** large club/player quality advantages compound strongly;
  managers do not yet reason about the first-leg aggregate. Retention acknowledges
  these limits rather than certifying empirical realism.
