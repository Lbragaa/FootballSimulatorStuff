## Foreign squads and identity

`rosters.py` loads every recorded club/player unchanged into an additive forward registry. IDs retain original identity, including archived #196 and active #354. Foreign clubs use `modeled:` plus a SHA256 digest of a Unicode-normalized, accent-insensitive club alias. Aliases collapse case/spacing/diacritics, not unrelated country-qualified clubs. Unique SQL constraints reject collisions and ambiguous duplicates. No contemporary real-world player source is queried.

There are 49 foreign clubs requiring squads: recorded Libertadores powers supply 23 club priors; the 26 additional Sudamericana clubs have group records/pots but no power column. Their **explicit proposed** prior is `68 + 2*(5-pot) + clamp(.15*(group_points-9), -1, 1)`. Existing Brazilian strengths and players are retained. Unknown Sudamericana nationalities remain null. Individual fictional names are visibly `Fictional CLUB POSITION-SLOT`; no generated player is represented as a historical real person.

Policy `fictional-squads-1.0.0`, fixed seed `task03b-fictional-rosters-v1`, club power and serialized world policy seed a local SHA256-derived MT19937 stream. Squads have 24–28 players: three GK, eight DF, seven MF, five FW plus one to five outfield reserves. OVR is a bounded distribution centered near club power minus two; role attributes, fitness 87–98 and morale 55–77 vary deterministically. Those distributions are modeling assumptions, not observations or empirical calibration. The current extension contains 1,276 modeled players.

Player IDs are `1_000_000_000 + int(SHA256(club_key + ':player:' + slot)[:12],16)`. This stays within exact JSON integer range and far from recorded IDs. Collisions fail initialization; IDs are not silently reallocated. Generated rows and source/seed/evidence provenance persist once in the candidate and are reused for every later match. Stable identity and a separate current `club_key` support future approved transfers without rewriting old appearances; no transfer is generated in this task. Modeled registration/contract windows cover the forward remainder of 2027 only and carry no invented salary transaction. All proposed roster evidence has `canonical_approval=False`.

Four concrete adapters handle separate schemas. League references use `league:ID`; CDB `cdb:QF-01:L1`; Libertadores `lib:LIB-R16-01:L1`; Sudamericana `sula:SULA-PO-01:L1`. No bare integer is treated as a global competition identity.

Recorded `season_rules`, Libertadores metadata and CDB plan metadata were inspected. They establish league/history conventions, known participants/draws and CDB's two legs through semifinals with a single final. They do **not** establish all knockout tie-break, suspension, final-venue or future continental draw rules. Consequently `knockout-proposal-1.0.0` explicitly proposes no away goals, direct penalties after tied two-leg aggregates, and 30-minute extra time then penalties for a single final. CDB future administrative home ordering uses the existing parent tie order; venue is unknown and the inherited match model still applies its home effect. Final neutrality/home advantage needs a commissioner/calibration decision before official use. These are test policies, not imported current regulations.

Extra time reuses chance distributions, finishing probabilities and postmatch metrics; it retains the regulation final eligible XI, models no additional ET cards/injuries/substitutions, and adds exact seconds/statistics. This is a disclosed first integration simplification. Shootouts use five alternating kicks with early stopping, then sudden death; a large safety bound fails closed rather than inventing a tiebreak winner. Shootout kicks never count as match goals, xG or player goals. The normal narration final whistle comes after resolution; regulation end and extra-time/shootout boundaries are explicit.

Known CDB next_tie progression is implemented. Continental later rounds have round-level capacity reservations but no approved participant draw mapping. They stay conditional after prior winners are known. Reaching one raises an exact unresolved qualifier/draw blocker, never skips it or silently creates pairings. The bounded September 30/R26 demonstration finishes before that decision. Tests also run through October 16 to verify return legs, aggregates and CDB quarterfinal/semifinal activation; a further continental-boundary test verifies the fail-closed stop.

## One chronological player world

`world_player_state` is the forward observation layer; the legacy `players` table and historical counters remain unchanged. Recovery is **1.5 fitness points per elapsed day**, clipped at 100, projected from the player's last observation. Match updates store fitness/form/morale at actual finish time. Intermediate projection is not an extra match/round bonus. Future kickoff snapshots are built after all earlier applicable completion transactions.

Actual dated main-league/Carioca, CDB leg performances and Sudamericana player appearances supply a chronological recent-performance window. CDB duplicates in the main fixture mirror are excluded; unknown-date rows are not invented. Existing source fitness/form remain checkpoint observations. Future form uses the same legacy-derived four-match weighting, now with actual forward appearances across all competitions. Coach continuity uses recorded recent appearances; no new hidden team familiarity bonus is introduced.

Proposed discipline starts forward counters at zero because historical suspension registers are absent. Three yellows trigger a one-match ban; a red triggers at least one match, additive if the yellow threshold also applies. Bans are served in the same competition, not by unrelated league/cup games. Known signing contract dates and registration/archive exclusions are enforced. Unknown historical bans and untracked contracts remain explicitly disclosed assumptions. New injuries are unavailable until five days after match completion; missing historical recovery dates stay unknown/unavailable. Explicit bounded training rows carry unique effect IDs and one-time consumed_fixture values; the old R25 training effect is not installed. New training or transfer policies need approved input/provenance before production use.

## Commit, simulate, apply, recover

1. Creation authenticates exact accepted DB SHA256, copies it twice into a fresh directory, and writes initialization policy/seed commitment before modeled-player draws. No accepted registry changes.
2. Every writer authenticates the development marker, supported policy/version, exact parent hash and distinct working/parent files. A byte-range Windows lock or POSIX flock coordinates processes and is released by the OS on a real crash. SQLite uses foreign keys and FULL synchronous commits.
3. A simultaneous kickoff group receives one pre-kickoff world hash and separate immutable snapshots/config/seed commitments. These rows commit **before** any lineup/match RNG. A club cannot be in overlapping fixtures. Pending sealed results are excluded from other matches' football-state hash.
4. Pure simulation and independent regulation audit run outside the apply transaction; validated full results persist separately. A crash resumes the same inputs/seeds or uses stored results. No replacement seed option exists.
5. Kickoff and completion are different agenda events. This matters for October 3's 18:00 and 19:00 matches: 19:00 starts before 18:00 finishes at 19:33. A group applies atomically at its maximum completion time, so all equal-kickoff fixtures use one coherent pre-state. Player availability/recovery still uses each match's actual end.
6. One transaction applies every group receipt, native/normalized result, events, performances, discipline, injuries, training consumption, standings and bracket change. Validation failure rolls the entire group back. Earlier completed groups remain committed; a multi-group run is resumable progress, not an all-or-nothing week. Commitments/results survive rollback intentionally.
7. Every transaction records complete keyed before/after images in a hash-chained operation log. Independent reversal must reconstruct the exact parent plus empty additive tables. Historical/schema checks, native result/aggregate checks and complete result accounting run before publication. Existing `world_finals` tables are not mistaken for new schema ownership.

Administrative obligations share the agenda. September through December month-end events use existing `payroll.obligations` and `book_payroll`; known contract periods/cent rounding and reserve constraints are reused. September's registered amount due is R$1,799,666.67, posted once on September 30; its prior accrued liability is not an additional debit. No revenue, owner capital, budget increase, transfer, award or foreign-club payroll is invented.

`world advance --until` processes events up to an explicit local fictional boundary; the clock records the last processed event, not an implicit fast-forward to the requested text. `--before-fixture` stops before that fixture's whole simultaneous group. It refuses clock rewind. Half-open in-progress worlds can be resumed but cannot be packaged until their committed batches finish.

## Audit, export and Hikari

Development packages reuse the existing atomic publisher, manifest, safe ZIP extraction and independent restoration. They contain the exact accepted parent, working database, `DEV-03B` metadata, initial commitment, policies/versions, snapshots/config/seeds, receipts, all events/performance/state changes, operation log, audit, roster provenance and narration cursors. The accepted package and approval receipt never change. Export refuses incomplete batches.

`save verify/restore` dispatches the explicitly registered development profile. It verifies strict payload/schema/parent/history/journal/accounting and then creates a fresh world from the exact parent and committed seed, replays the entire bounded agenda and compares every logical table (apart from portable cursor positions, which are independently checked). Re-sealing a manifest cannot authorize altered history or unapproved model behavior. Runtime versions may yield different SQLite/ZIP layout bytes; CI compares the logical world hash, as well as original accepted hashes. This is corruption/provenance validation, not a cryptographic signature or a power-loss guarantee for cloud sync storage.

`DEV-03B` always returns `acceptance_eligible=False`; the existing promotion API rejects it. Changing its label to 047 fails the strict profile. A future official profile/authorization workflow must be separately implemented and reviewed.

Hikari receives a pregame-only preview, then `world narrate DIRECTORY --fixture KEY --count 1`. A persistent result-bound cursor releases only that match's next events, stripping future shot resolution and withholding final statistics until the final whistle. It does not expose unrelated future results. Complete world progress, including the noncanonical Vasco result and derived table, is explicitly private diagnostic context. Local files are not encrypted/access-controlled; direct opening bypasses the normal spoiler gate. The public engineering summary exposes counts, validation and hashes, not the Vasco result.
